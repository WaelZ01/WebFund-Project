Welcome

Many ICT and Cybersecurity students finish their studies with strong technical knowledge but little guidance on how to turn it into a job offer. Advice online is often generic and not written for students in Bahrain.

Career Prep Hub is an informational website that brings practical, specific guidance into one place.

Project overview

  • Purpose: help students prepare for internships and graduate jobs.
  • Audience: ICT and Cybersecurity students at Bahrain Polytechnic.
  • Solution: career-path information, skills guidance, CV advice and interview preparation.
  • Category: Informational.
